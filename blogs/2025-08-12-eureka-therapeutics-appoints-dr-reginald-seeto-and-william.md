Emeryville, Calif., August 12th, 2025 – Eureka Therapeutics, Inc., a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering safer and more effective T-cell therapies, today announced the appointment of Dr. Reginald Seeto and William S. Solari III to its Board of Directors.
